# Taggy PRD (Version 1.0)
1. Product Name

Taggy

2. Vision

    Taggy helps people learn skills to a level where they can earn from them by growing alongside a community.

`Gym buddies for skill growth.`

    People often quit online learning because they lack accountability, structure, and peers. Taggy aims to solve that.

3. Problem Statement

**Current learning platforms have several issues:**
    Learning is often isolated.
    Courses provide information but not accountability.
    Users jump between resources without a clear roadmap.
    Motivation fades over time.
    Learners struggle to stay consistent long enough to become employable.

4. Target Users
**Primary Users**
    People learning skills online.

Examples:
    Web development learners
    AI/ML learners
    UI/UX learners
    Data analysts

**Secondary Users**
Students preparing for placements.
Examples:
    DSA preparation
    Aptitude preparation
    Interview preparation
    Future Users (Not MVP)

**Competitive exam aspirants.**
Examples:
    GATE
    UPSC
    CAT

5. Core Value Proposition

**Taggy combines:**
    Structured learning paths,
    Community accountability,
    Small support groups,
    Progress tracking,
to help users remain consistent until they achieve real outcomes.

6. Success Metrics (MVP)

**Measure whether Taggy is actually helping.**
    User Metrics
        Daily Active Users (DAU)
        Weekly Active Users (WAU)
    Engagement Metrics
        Average study hours logged per week
        Average streak length
        Percentage of milestones completed
    Retention Metrics
        Day 7 retention
        Day 30 retention

7. MVP Features
**Authentication**
Users can:
    Sign up with Google.
    Log in securely.
    View and edit profile information.

**Skills**
Users can:
Select a skill to pursue.
Join the official community for that skill.

**Rules:**
Free users → 1 active skill.
Premium users → multiple skills (future).

**Milestones**
Each skill has predefined milestones.
Users can:
View upcoming milestones.
Mark milestones as complete.
Postpone milestones.

Users cannot:
Permanently skip milestones.

**Progress Tracking**
Users can:
Log study hours.
Track completed milestones.
Maintain streaks.


**Pods**
Small accountability groups.
Rules:
Maximum 7 members.
One active pod per user.
Join by request.
Pod owners can remove members.

**Community Features**
Users can:
Participate in skill community discussions.
Join channels inside communities.
Chat within pods.

**Reporting**
Users can report:
Spam,
Abuse,
Inappropriate content.

Admins review reports manually.

8. Non-MVP Features
These are intentionally postponed.
Direct Messages
AI-generated quizzes
AI evaluation systems
Recommendation engines
Video calling
Job boards
Competitive exam support
AI mock interviews (Premium)

9. Constraints
Technical
Backend-first development.
Monorepo architecture.
Production mindset from Day 1.
Product


10. Guiding Principles
    Accountability over content
    Taggy does not aim to replace courses.
    It helps users finish them.
    Progress over perfection
    Small consistent actions matter more than occasional bursts of motivation.
    Community over competition
    The goal is support and growth, not unhealthy comparison.
